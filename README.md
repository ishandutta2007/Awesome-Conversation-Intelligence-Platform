# Top Conversation Intelligence Platform Ecosystem 🌟

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects** 💡  
*Focusing on Meeting Recording, Voice Transcription, AI Sales Insights, and Automated Summarization*

**Last Updated: September 2026** 📅

---

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** in the field of **Conversation Intelligence**. These tools help sales, customer service, and product teams automatically record meetings, transcribe conversations, extract action items, analyze sentiment & competitive intelligence, and turn call data into actionable revenue insights.

---

## 📊 Market Size & Industry Insights

> 💡 **Estimated Market Size**: The global conversation intelligence and AI meeting assistant market size is projected to reach **$35B+ USD** by 2026 (CAGR > 22%).  
> ⚖️ **Concentration Level**: The industry is **Highly Fragmented**. Although top Enterprise players like Gong and ZoomInfo Chorus lead the market, many innovative products have emerged in SMB/PLG (Fireflies, tl;dv, Fathom) as well as self-hosted/local-first open-source ecosystems (Meetily, Vexa), with no "winner-takes-all" monopoly formed yet.

## 🛠️ Architectural Blueprints (Best Open-Source Stacks)

For teams looking to build custom, privacy-first, or self-hosted conversation intelligence systems, the recommended stack is:
- **Audio Capture & Transcription**: [Meetily](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meeting-minutes) or [WhisperX](https://github.com/m-bain/whisperX)
- **Multi-Platform Meeting Bot Integration**: [Vexa](https://github.com/Vexa-ai/vexa)
- **Enterprise Multi-Tenancy Architecture & Security**: [Nora](https://github.com/sf0rzin/nora)
- **Advanced Dialogue Analytics & Graphs**: [CEREBRO](https://github.com/officialarghya29/MindShift) + [SideQuest](https://github.com/aechemm/sidequest)
